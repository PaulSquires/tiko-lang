## Who this book is for

For programmers who already know a language and want tiko. It does not assume you know BASIC,
and it does not assume you have met FreeBASIC. Where tiko differs from FreeBASIC in a way that
would surprise someone who knows it, a sidebar says so.

## The version this book describes

Every program in this book was compiled and run, on Windows and on Linux, with one pinned

repository.
